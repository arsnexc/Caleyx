The full method
  • 01

    Lot-code certificates

    Every production run is assayed for identity and potency and screened for heavy metals by an independent laboratory. The certificate is published against the lot code printed on your pack — if the number does not resolve, write to us.

  • 02

    Dose and rationale

    Each product page states the exact dose per serving, the form of the ingredient, and the reason for both. No proprietary blends, so nothing on the label requires a legend to decode.

  • 03

    The label, in full

    Capsules are vegetable cellulose. Powders are the ingredient and nothing else. Droppers are MCT and the vitamins they carry. What is on the pack is on the page — and vice versa.
